Swimming

I have enjoyed swimming my entire life. Ever since I could remember I loved to swim.

From elementary to middle school I would always love when our physical education unit would cover swimming.

It was only until my senior year of high school that I would begin to swim competitively. I would have begun earlier but COVID-19 had made that more difficult. However, even with only spending one season as a swimmer I made great friendships with the others on the team, and I also strengthened previous friendships that I still hold near and dear to me. I still carry around a photo inside of my phone case of me and my buddies.
